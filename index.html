About Prerna

( 01 )

We believe meaningful change begins when people come together around a cause.

Prerna is imagined as a community-first organisation: small teams, long stays, measurable outcomes. We work where the need is and we stay until the need changes.

Every programme is built with local partners — teachers, health workers, coaches — so the work continues long after a project closes.

What we do

( 02 )

Three focus areas. One belief: potential is everywhere — opportunity is not.

Each programme runs on its own, and stronger together: a child who learns, heals and plays is a child who stays in school.

  • 01 Education Learning centres, scholarships, mentorship ↗
  • 02 Healthcare Health camps, maternal care, health literacy ↗
  • 03 Sports Coaching, kit, safe playgrounds ↗
Programme
01

Education

Open doors to learning.

Scholarships, neighbourhood learning centres and mentorship that keep children in school — and curious about what comes next. We fund the boring, brilliant things: a desk, a lamp, a teacher who stays.

  • After-school learning centres
  • Girl-child scholarships
  • Digital & library access
  • Teacher support
Programme
02

Healthcare

Build healthier communities.

Care that travels to the doorstep: health camps with local clinics, maternal support, and the kind of health education that prevents the emergency in the first place.

  • Mobile health camps
  • Maternal & child care
  • Nutrition programmes
  • Health worker training

03

Sports

Give potential a place to grow.

Coaches, kit and safe spaces that turn playgrounds into pathways — building discipline, teamwork and joy, one season at a time.

  • Community coaching
  • Girls' sport leagues
  • Safe play spaces
  • Equipment & kit

Impact stories

( 04 )

Featured story · Education

A new beginning through education.

When her school closed, Meera's days went quiet. A Prerna learning centre reopened the door — a desk, a library, a teacher who noticed her. Two terms later she is top of her class, and teaching her younger brother his letters.

HealthcareField note

Care that travels to the doorstep.

In villages where the nearest clinic is an hour away, the clinic now arrives on two wheels — with blood pressure cuffs, medicine and a midwife.

SportsField note

A field, a team, a future.

Four evenings a week, the empty lot behind the school becomes the strongest room in the neighbourhood — no walls, no fees, one shared ball.
